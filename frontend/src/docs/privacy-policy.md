## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how QWShortLink collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use the service.

QWShortLink is a free, non-commercial, open-source URL shortening service operated as a personal hobby project.

By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information Collected

### Account Registration Data

To create an account, users must provide:

* Username
* Email address
* Password

Users may optionally provide:

* First name
* Last name

### Google Sign-In

Users may alternatively register or sign in using Google authentication.

When using Google Sign-In, the service may receive information associated with the following Google scopes:

* `openid`
* `email`
* `profile`

The information received may include your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information made available through those scopes.

### Email Verification and Password Reset Emails

Email verification is required for all accounts.

The service sends transactional emails for:

* Account email verification
* Password reset requests

The service does not send advertising, marketing, or promotional emails.

### Link Data

When users create shortened links, the service stores the information necessary to create and manage those links.

### Click Counts

The service records the number of times a shortened link is accessed.

The service does not intentionally collect IP addresses, geographic location information, or similar visitor-identifying information for click counting purposes.

### Cookies and Authentication

The service uses a strictly necessary authentication cookie to maintain logged-in user sessions.

This cookie:

* Is used only for authentication and session management
* Is configured as an HttpOnly cookie where supported
* Is not used for advertising
* Is not used for analytics
* Is not used for cross-site tracking

## How Information Is Used

Information collected by the service is used solely to:

* Create and maintain user accounts
* Authenticate users
* Verify email addresses
* Process password reset requests
* Allow users to create and manage shortened URLs
* Maintain service functionality
* Count link visits
* Protect the service against abuse and misuse

## Data Retention

Account information and link data are retained only while an account remains active or in accordance with the verification process described below.

When a user registers but does not complete email verification, the account and associated data are stored temporarily for up to three (3) days. If the email address is not verified within this period, the account and all associated data are scheduled for deletion from the database.

Users may delete their accounts at any time.

When an account is deleted (either by the user or automatically due to unverified status), associated account information and shortened links are removed from the service database.

The operator does not maintain backups of user account data or shortened links.

## Account Deletion

Users may delete their accounts at any time through the service.

Upon account deletion:

* Account information is deleted from the database
* Associated shortened links are deleted from the database
* The account can no longer be accessed

Deletion is intended to take effect immediately.

## Data Security

The operator takes reasonable measures to protect stored information, including practices such as:

* Using TLS/HTTPS for data transmission
* Storing passwords using secure hashing methods

However, no method of electronic storage, transmission, or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The operator cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information stored or transmitted through the service.

### General Note on Third Parties

Each third-party provider listed above processes data only to the extent necessary for the functionality they provide. Their use of data may be governed by their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

The service is intended only for individuals who are at least 18 years old.

Persons under the age of 18 may not create accounts or use the service.

The operator does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

If the operator becomes aware that an account belongs to a person under 18 years of age, the account and associated data may be removed without notice.

## Link Safety

Before redirecting users to a destination URL, the service displays an intermediary page showing the full destination address.

This feature is intended to help users identify potentially harmful or misleading links before proceeding.

### Abuse Reports

To report a suspicious, harmful, illegal, fraudulent, or otherwise problematic shortened link, please send an email containing:

* The shortened link or shortcode
* A brief description of the issue
* Any additional information that may assist in reviewing the report
